How to use this on an iPad

Open this page in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, so it opens like an app. Trace the dotted number with a finger or Apple Pencil. Numbers start on the top line and sit on the dark blue baseline. Coloured numbers show which line to make first, and arrows show which way to go. Tap Show me to watch the number being made, and tap 1 2 3 Steps to hide or show the steps. Tap I traced it when you are done: the green dot is only saved when the tracing stays on the line and covers the whole number. Progress stays on this iPad.
